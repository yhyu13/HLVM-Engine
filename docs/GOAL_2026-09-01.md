## Phase 4d — Taste-score competition (added 2026-09-02)
Scope: derive a numeric 0–100 *taste* score for rendered frames
distinct from the test pass/fail contract. Multiple agent profiles
compete overnight to push the score up.

Why: validators catch *correctness*. They don't catch "looks
wrong" (uniform gray GI, hot pixels, banding). The v236 sky-bounce
incident (validator passed, dump looked bad) is the canonical
example. A taste score with explicit dimensions + per-dimension
breakdown turns "this looks bad" into a measurable diagnosis.

**Success:**
- `docs/TASTE_SCORE.md` defines 6 dimensions (PBR, light
  transport, signal/noise, composition, material, temporal) with
  weights and computable sub-metrics.
- `docs/COMPETITION_HARNESS.md` defines roles, state machine,
  queue ranking.
- `docs/agents/dispatcher_competition.md` is the cron prompt.
- Cron job registered with `enabled_toolsets: ["terminal","file"]`
  and `deliver: "origin"`.
- First overnight run produces a morning digest with at least 1
  scored cycle.
