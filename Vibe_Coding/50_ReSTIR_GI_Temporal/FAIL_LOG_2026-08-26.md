## 1. What actually happened

- The Sponza default camera `(0, 2.5, 18) -> (0, 2.5, -10)` (set 2026-08-10)
  sat 7.6 m OUTSIDE the outer `bricks` shell (scene z max ~10.4). With the
  turntable's old default yaw=90 the building's long axis rotates into Z and
  the camera ends up inside the end wall. Depending on the run the frame was
  either a smooth gray gradient (exterior wall + ambient) or open sky.
- Every validator gate is a statistical property that a smooth gradient
  satisfies: black-ratio, color-variance (a gradient has std), cell-variance
  (a gradient's cell means differ), temporal stability (a static wall is
  perfectly stable), noise/bias gates (wall vs itself), M accumulation and
  firefly bounds (scene-independent). Proof below.
- Consequently ALL Sponza evidence from 2026-08-10 onward is void:
  - v213 "real-time ReSTIR screenshots (static + rotating)" — walls.
  - v233 before/after noise-gate calibration ("post-denoise CV 0.24 vs 0.25")
    — measured on a wall; the calibration does not transfer to real content.
  - v215 "Sponza ReSTIR vs 256-frame path-trace reference, MAE 0.029, rel err
    5.4%" — **wall vs wall**. Dump groups `20260826_215547` / `_215559` (the
    ReSTIR and PT-reference 256-frame runs) both contain zero scene content
    (saturated-pixel fraction 0.0000).
  - v215 "default rotating Sponza 24.3 ms, M 9.48/10, validator 9/9 PASS" —
    the 9/9 was computed on the wall framing.
- Cornell Box evidence is VALID (camera inside the box; red/green walls and
  color bleed visible; saturated-pixel fraction 0.58).

## 4. What the real content exposed (OPEN — next session's priority)

With the camera fixed, the first-ever real-content validation runs:

| config | result | failing gate |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell 48f | **9/9 PASS** | — |
| Sponza static 48f | 8/9 | bias bound: median spatial/gi_raw = **0.061** |
| Sponza rotating 48f | 8/9 | noise_reduction: denoised CV / gi_raw CV = **1.095** |

Static float stats: gi_raw mean [0.104, 0.093, 0.083], spatial mean
[0.039, 0.033, 0.027] — the ReSTIR path carries only ~37% of the raw
estimate's mean energy, and the spatial dump is visibly a black "starfield"
(sparse bright speckle; rotating is the same texture at higher density).
M saturates (9.95/10), so temporal reuse runs — but the estimate is dark.

**Ruled out:** the M cap. A/B with `HLVM_RGI_MAXM=30` on static: bias median
0.063 (unchanged), M mean 29.8 — the energy loss is not the v215 M_MAX=10
alignment.

**Not ruled out (candidates, in suspicion order):**
1. The v232/v233 spatial-pass changes (Jacobian clamp 1e-4..1e2, W/w_sum
   clamps, wave-anti-firefly clamp) — never validated on real content; the
   wave clamp divides by `WaveGetLaneCount()-1` even when few lanes are
   active, which can over-suppress (clamps down only, matches the symptom).
2. The v215 `inout FReservoir r` TemporalResample2 fix: on walls it only had
   to reproduce a uniform field; on real content a pairwise-MIS
   normalization error (m_curr/m_prev weights) would systematically darken.
   Cornell passes at 7.4% vs PT, so if this is the cause it is
   content-dependent (textures/scale/lighting).
3. gi_raw includes the Sponza ambient-hack term while the reservoir path does
   not — but that explains a mild uniform ratio, not a starfield at 0.06
   median.

## 5. Lessons

- **Statistical gates cannot substitute for looking at the image.** One
  visual inspection of `sponza_default_display.png` would have caught this at
  any point since 08-10. Evidence pairs in a FIX_LOG are only as good as
  their pixels.
- **Every validator needs a semantic content gate** — a property the failure
  mode cannot fake. Variance floors measure *that* pixels differ, not *what*
  they show.
- **A fix without a committed FAIL_LOG is an orphan.** The camera fix sat
  uncommitted for 50 minutes referencing a FAIL_LOG that did not exist; a
  session boundary would have lost the entire story.
