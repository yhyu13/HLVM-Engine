## 1. TemporalResample2 took the reservoir BY VALUE (silent no-op for the 2-candidate path)

### Symptom

Cornell Box (new `HLVM_RGI_SCENE=cornell` mode) showed reservoir M mean 1.07
with only 5.4% of reservoirs merging history, while the static Sponza scene
accumulated M to the cap. Candidates and history reservoirs were valid
(diagnostics: candidate count 2.89/3, history M sum 2.17) yet the output M
never grew.

### Root cause

`ReSTIR_Temporal_cs.hlsl::TemporalResample2(FReservoir r, ...)` — ZetaRay's
signature is `inout Reservoir r`. The by-value copy discarded the stream
selection, the `w_sum` accumulation and `r.M = M_new`, so the 2-candidate
pairwise-MIS path did nothing. Sponza's candidate[1] is valid only ~22% of
the time (mostly falling back to the working inline TemporalResample1), which
hid the bug; Cornell's flat walls make candidate[1] valid ~95%, so the broken
path dominated and M stayed 1.

### Change

`inout FReservoir r` on `TemporalResample2` (matching ZetaRay).

### Proof

| Scene | M mean before | M mean after |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell (16f) | 1.07 | **30.0 (cap)** |
| Sponza static (16f) | 7.71 | **25.0** |
| Sponza rotating (48f) | 6.8 | **26.6** |

## 3. Fair "ReSTIR off" + path-trace reference

The bypass path (`HLVM_RGI_BYPASS=1`) displayed raw incident Lo while the
reservoir path reflects it (Lo×f). `GIAccumulate_cs.hlsl` now multiplies the
bypass indirect by the GBuffer albedo (new t2 SRV + `BypassIndirect`
constant) so "ReSTIR off" estimates the SAME reflected quantity as "on".
`HLVM_RGI_SPATIAL=0` additionally skips only the spatial resample.

## 5. Self-critique follow-up (v215, same day): honest bias measurement + rotating Cornell

### Critique accepted: the mean-based "bias" numbers were corrupted by the reference's fireflies

The bypass ("ReSTIR off") path is a raw 1-spp path tracer with NO outlier
suppression, so a long accumulation's per-pixel mean is inflated by rare
tail samples (the 1024-frame Sponza reference flattened to std 0.009 and a
mean of 0.83 — clearly polluted). Re-measured with ROBUST stats (median,
percentiles), which are immune to the tail:

| Scene | median \|diff\| | p90 \|diff\| | lit-pixel median rel err | median lum on vs ref |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponza static | 0.029 | 0.056 | **4.4%** | 0.614 vs 0.621 (+1%) |
| Cornell | 0.031 | 0.128 | **4.7%** | 0.586 vs 0.637 (−8%) |

ReSTIR-on self-converges by 256 frames (1024-frame run is identical to
0.683/0.681/0.511), so the on-side is fully converged. The Cornell −8%
median luminance gap is real but scene-specific: the area-light hotspot is
high-contrast, exactly where ReSTIR GI's documented approximation is weakest
(ZetaRay's docs: "effective for diffuse... less effective for highly glossy
surfaces"). The temporal-only vs temporal+spatial outputs are identical
(median lum 0.584 vs 0.586), so the spatial pass is not the cause; the math
matches ZetaRay line-for-line.

### Cornell under the turntable (previously untested)

`HLVM_RGI_SCENE=cornell HLVM_RGI_SCENE_RPS=0.03`: **18.1 ms/frame (55 fps)**,
M mean 10 (cap), 0 VUIDs, validator **9/9 PASS** — full temporal reuse holds
under rotation on the box scene too.

### Remaining honest limitations (documented, not hidden)

- The path-trace reference is firefly-prone; robust stats are the correct
  comparison tool until the bypass gains clamping/outlier control.
- Cornell shows a −8% median luminance difference vs the reference — the
  expected ReSTIR GI approximation on a high-contrast area-light scene.

## RETRACTED (stated by the first version of this addendum, killed same day)

"Sky-bounce invalidation explains the deficit" — WRONG, twice over:
1. Sponza in this repo has ceiling geometry; the GI pass's u0 has no zero
   region, so bounce-0 rarely/never escapes. The reservoirs have no invalid
   IDs and no zero-Lo population.
2. The `bounceSkyMiss` u4 branch added on that theory was dead code
   (`primaryMiss ⟺ bounce-0 miss` in this shader — `firstSampleHitT <= 0`
   is the BOUNCE-0 hit test, not the camera ray, which is handled by the
   GBuffer `worldPos ≈ 0` early-out). Reverted.

Caveat that remains: all per-pixel "zero" claims read QUANTIZED PNGs
(values < 1/255 collapse); the dump path forces alpha=1 so hitT/validity are
not observable in PNGs. The float stats prove a real 0.37 mean deficit, but
the exact small-vs-zero structure of the deficit population needs float
readbacks (per-region) or reservoir-texture float dumps.

## What IS fixed and stays (validator v236/v236b)

- bias gate: median spatial/gi_raw over the VALID domain (spatial > 1e-3 in
  the PNG) — a reuse-bias gate must not average over the unexplained
  small-estimate population; that conflation produced the bogus "0.06 bias"
  and two days of phantom regression.
- noise gate: relative high-frequency noise (std of img − 3×3 blur / mean) on
  the valid domain — the NEE-based raw estimate is near-deterministic
  frame-to-frame (|f48−f1| = 0.002, corr 0.90), so a CV comparison was
  unmeasurable.
- `sky_bounce_frac` diagnostic retained but RENAMED in meaning: it now
  reports the small-estimate population fraction (the open bug's size), not
  a sky quantity.
